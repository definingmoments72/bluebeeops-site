Part 1

Coverage Service ($149 a month)

Coverage Service is our only live plan. It answers your missed and after-hours calls in your shop's name, gets the caller's name, number and what they need, and texts it to you right away.

How it works

What does Coverage actually do?

It answers your missed and after-hours calls in your shop's name. It gets the caller's name, number and what they need, then texts it to you right away so you can call back as soon as you're free. Nothing changes about how you run your shop.

Does it answer nights, weekends and holidays?

Yes. It answers any time, including nights, weekends and holidays. It's still not an emergency line, though (see the next questions).

Does it book jobs or appointments?

No. Coverage takes the details and texts you, and you call the customer back. Booking starts with Intake Service, which is coming soon.

Is this an emergency line?

No. Blue Bee isn't an emergency line, a dispatcher, or 24-hour emergency coverage. On an urgent call it takes the details and texts you right away, and you call back.

Is there a call limit, or a charge per call?

There are no per-call charges. It's one flat price, built for one shop's missed and after-hours calls, and if your volume ever runs far past normal, we'll reach out to you first.

Your callers

Will my customers know it's not me?

Yes, and that's on purpose. It answers in your shop's name, says plainly that the caller has reached an automated assistant, and never pretends to be a person. It takes the details, and you make the callback yourself.

What will callers hear when it picks up?

A short greeting in your shop's name that we write together on your setup call, with your shop's name said the way it should sound. It tells callers it's an automated assistant, then asks what they need. The call stays short.

What about robocalls and sales pitches?

Coverage screens out junk calls, so robocalls and sales pitches don't keep your phone buzzing.

Will my callers get a text back?

Yes, if they want one. After it takes the message, Coverage offers the caller one text confirming their message reached you. It's optional and only goes out if they say yes. You still make the callback.

Does it remember repeat callers?

That isn't a Coverage feature. Coverage takes each caller's name, number and what they need, texts it to you, and every call shows up in your daily report. If the demo greeted you by name on a second call, that's part of the demo only.

Can it talk with callers in Spanish?

It's English for now.

What you get

What's in the text I get after a call?

The caller's name, their number and what they need, sent right after the call so you know who called and why. Here's an example with a made-up caller:

Missed call caught

Name: Dana R.

Number: (360) 555-01••

Needs: water heater leaking in the garage, asked for a callback this evening.

What's the "mood note"?

Every call comes with a short note on the caller's mood, like "Stressed, cold house with two kids" or "Calm, comparing a few quotes."

Do I get a report?

Yes, every day, for as long as you're with Blue Bee. It lists everything Blue Bee caught, plus a note on each caller's mood, so you can count for yourself whether it's worth it. The full report comes with Coverage; it isn't held back for higher plans.

Can my family or best customers be put straight through to me?

Coming soon. For now, Coverage takes the details and texts you.

Setup

How does setup work?

It's one 15-minute call with Jase. Together we write your greeting, set up forwarding for your missed and after-hours calls, and test it before it ever goes live. Jase sets up every shop himself, so you won't be handed off to a call center or a ticket queue, and he's glad to stop by the shop.

Do I have to change or give up my phone number?

No. You keep your number, and we set up forwarding so your missed and after-hours calls reach your Blue Bee line.

Will it work with my phone?

Yes. It works with any carrier and any phone.

Pricing

How much does it cost?

$149 a month, with $0 setup.

Is $149 a month worth it?

In a 2025 CallRail survey of 1,000 U.S. consumers, 42% said they leave a voicemail when a business doesn't pick up, and 78% said they've given up on a business after a call went unanswered. Coverage costs less than half of one typical service call, about $341 (Angi national cost guides; that's the price of the call, not profit, and a bit more for HVAC and electrical). Your daily report shows what it caught, so you can judge for yourself.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes, once the other plans open. Intake Service ($249 a month) and Estimate Request Service ($349 a month plus $300–$500 setup) are both coming soon. You can start with Coverage and decide later, once the reports show you what it's catching.
