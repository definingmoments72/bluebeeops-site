# Part 1: Coverage Service ($149 a month)

Coverage Service is our only live plan. It answers your missed and after-hours calls in your shop's name, gets the caller's name, number and what they need, and texts it to you right away.

## How it works

### 1. What does Coverage actually do?
It answers your missed and after-hours calls in your shop's name. It gets the caller's name, number and what they need, then texts it to you right away so you can call back as soon as you're free. Nothing changes about how you run your shop.

## Your callers

### 7. Will my customers know it's not me?
Yes, and that's on purpose. It answers in your shop's name, says plainly that the caller has reached an automated assistant, and never pretends to be a person. It takes the details, and you make the callback yourself.

### 8. What will callers hear when it picks up?
A short greeting in your shop's name that we write together on your setup call, with your shop's name said the way it should sound. It tells callers it's an automated assistant and that the call may be recorded, then asks what they need. The call stays short.

### 9. What about robocalls and sales pitches?
Coverage screens out junk calls, so robocalls and sales pitches don't keep your phone buzzing.

## What you get

### 13. What's in the text I get after a call?
The caller's name, their number and what they need, sent right after the call so you know who called and why. Here's an example with a made-up caller:

> Missed call caught
> Name: Dana R.
> Number: (360) 555-01••
> Needs: water heater leaking in the garage, asked for a callback this evening.

### 14. What's the "mood note"?
Every call comes with a short note on the caller's mood, like "Stressed, cold house with two kids" or "Calm, comparing a few quotes." The caller is never asked about it, and it's never said on the call.

### 15. Do I get a report?
Yes, every day, for as long as you're with Blue Bee. It lists everything Blue Bee caught, plus a note on each caller's mood, so you can count for yourself whether it's worth it. The full report comes with Coverage; it isn't held back for higher plans.

## Setup

### 17. How does setup work?
It's one 15-minute call with Jase. Together we write your greeting, set up forwarding for your missed and after-hours calls, and test it before it ever goes live. Jase sets up every shop himself, so you won't be handed off to a call center or a ticket queue, and he's glad to stop by the shop.

### 18. Do I have to change or give up my phone number?
No. You keep your number. Your missed and after-hours calls simply forward to your Blue Bee line, so there's no number transfer to wait on.
